Just type

The omnibar understands short commands and plain questions. No AI needed; an optional local model reads whole sentences.

Private by design

Firn has no servers of its own. There is nothing to sign up for and nothing that phones home.

Your files

Notes are plain Markdown in a folder you pick. Local calendars and contacts are standard .ics and .vcf files beside them.

Your accounts

Calendars and contacts sync straight with your own Nextcloud, iCloud, Google or CalDAV server. Passwords stay in the system keychain.

Your computer

The optional language model runs on this computer through Ollama. Nothing you type is sent anywhere else.

Presenting mode

Plug in a projector and private events, contacts and todos step aside until you're done.

Works offline

Everything keeps working without a connection and syncs when you're back.

No tracking

The app collects nothing. This website uses no cookies.

macOS

macOS 11 or later, Apple Silicon and Intel.

Download Firn for Mac

Firn isn't notarized by Apple yet, so the first start needs one extra step:

  1. Open the downloaded file and drag Firn into Applications.
  2. Open Firn. macOS says it can't check the app; click Done.
  3. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway next to Firn, then confirm.

After that Firn opens like any other app, and updates keep working.

Questions

Is Firn really free?

Yes, for now and without ads. If people find it useful, a paid option may come later, such as end-to-end encrypted sync for notes. What you use today stays free.

Where is my data?

Notes are Markdown files in the folder you choose. Calendars, todos and contacts stay with your account (Nextcloud, iCloud, Google or another CalDAV server), or as files in your notes folder if you keep them on this computer. Firn itself stores nothing elsewhere.

Is there a phone app?

Not yet. Because Firn uses standard calendars and contacts, your phone's own apps see the same events, todos and people. Notes can sync through Nextcloud and open in any Markdown app.

Do I need the AI part?

No. Search, commands, questions like "when is my next dentist?" and everything else work without it. If you install Ollama, Firn can also read full sentences such as "lunch with Sarah next Tuesday at 12:30". It runs on your computer.

Can I bring my notes from another app?

Firn imports Joplin exports, and any folder of Markdown files (Obsidian, for example) works as it is.

Why does my Mac warn me about Firn?

Apple charges developers to notarize apps, and Firn isn't notarized yet. The app is the same either way; the three steps above tell macOS you trust it.
